![Roxie Ayers Evans Medkief, about 1910](/races/em-roxie-ayers-evans-medkief.jpg)

![Roxie Medkief and Ernest Clyde](/races/em-roxie-medkief-ernest-clyde.jpg)

![A handwritten note from Roxie](/races/em-roxies-note.jpg)

*Made July 3, 1938. Weight 100. I was 70 Aug 20, 1938. Was borned in Gilmer Co, Ga in 1868 Aug 20. Your Mother, Charlie. This for you and Grayce. Was married to your Father Aug 13, 1885. You was borned Sept 4th 1886 in Gilmer Co Ga.*

![Marriage License — Evans and Ayers](/races/em-marriage-license-evans-ayers.jpg)

*Gilmer County, Marriage Records, Book 3, p. 173. State of Georgia. To any Judge, Justice of the Peace, or Gilmer County Minister of the Gospel: you are hereby authorized to join John B. Evans and Roxella Ayers in the Holy State of Matrimony, according to the Constitution and Laws of this State, and for so doing this shall be your license. And you are hereby required to return this to me with your certificate hereon of the fact and date of the Marriage. Given under my hand and Seal this \_\_ day of August 1885. J.L. Allen, Ordinary. Georgia, Gilmer County. I certify that John B. Evans and Roxella Ayers were join in Matrimony by me, this 13 day of August Eighteen Hundred and Eighty five. J.P. Chastine, C.M.G.*

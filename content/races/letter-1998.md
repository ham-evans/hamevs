We are enjoying our 4th year of retirement as well as the life in Scottsdale, Arizona. I've spent some time casually working on the Genealogy of both the Evans and Hayes family, and have made some good progress using the computer and the Internet primarily. I'll send you all an update in a few months.

Marily is now working at the Evans Collection, a Native American jewelry wholesale business owned by Pam Evans. Marily, Pam and Colleen have a great time together.

Bruce and Anita welcomed the fourth member of their family, Preston, in July. They are still in Moscow, Idaho. We'll be joining them for Christmas this year.

Tim and Karen are now Dad & Mom to Hamilton (who inaugurates the name!), born in December 1997. Tim changed law firms this year, and they moved to the Los Angeles area. We miss having them close by, but at least they are only a 6-hour drive away.

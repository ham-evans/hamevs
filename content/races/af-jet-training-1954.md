## Transcription

### Aeronautical Orders — September 29, 1954

Headquarters, Flying Training Air Force (ATRC), Waco, Texas. Aeronautical Orders Number 69.

The 3640th Pilot Training Squadron (former STOOFF Class 55-B) who were previously rated as Student Officers are re-rated as 1st Lt AERO. Evans, David D. is listed among the 2nd Lieutenants.

### Class Roster with Scores — October 12, 1954

Headquarters, 3640th Pilot Training Wing (Base), Laredo Air Force Base, Texas. Flight Actions Memorandum Number 295.

The FWD 3640th Student Squadron (Former STOOFF Class 55-B) who held CONF DIR are AND change of 2nd AFSC, effective 13 Oct 54, AFSC INDOC is re-listed as 1st AEO.

Evans, David D. is listed with AFSN AO3011940, with scores and grades from the program. Listed as "2ndLt" and promoted to Pilot rating.

### Specialty Training Assignments — October 22, 1954

Upon completion of primary jet training, Evans and others had the option of advanced training at Nellis AFB (requiring an additional 5-year commitment) or specialty school. Evans and his buddy Jim Leeth chose Armament Systems Officer school at Lowry AFB, Denver, Colorado, along with several others in their class.

For Ted & Esther

My Congratulations on your 50th Anniversary!!

You guys have had a great 50, with a lot of hard work and plenty of blessings with family, grandchildren, and good times that you have enjoyed together.

Unfortunately I never had a good opportunity to really get to know Esther, however Ted was one of my dearest friends throughout high school and into college. So I don't have any really good 'stories' to share of the two of them together.

I do have fond memories of visiting with Ted and his family, and chumming around with him and often with one of our other good friends, Everett (George) Smith. The below snapshot is one I took at a 'guys-only' picnic somewhere above Durango, Colorado as I recall. At any rate it gives one a look at our early days, well before girls were quite as important! I also think we were all a bit shy in those days.

![Ted and friend at a picnic above Durango, Colorado](/races/ted-esther-picnic.jpg)

My best regards on this special occasion!

Don Evans

Dear Family and Friends,

Best wishes for a happy Christmas and New Year. Here's a short note about our past year.

Marily (2 years) continues to receive a major share of our attention and energy. She's progressing very well, for which we're most thankful. She goes to a parent-teacher nursery school, and attends weekly physical therapy sessions, where they are working to strengthen her legs right now. Hopefully, she'll learn to walk, with crutches, during the next year or so. Meanwhile, she gets around nicely at home by crawling. Fortunately, she is very bright and literally talks our ears off.

Bruce (9) and Tim (10) are active 4th and 5th graders. They both do well in school (inherited, no doubt) and are active in our local YMCA-organized sports programs (baseball, flag football, and basketball).

Nancy has her hands full with the above characters, and is now in process of reoutfitting our living room as well (at long last!). I'm still at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, and am now Deputy Manager of the Liquid Propulsion Section. I spend most of my spare time in maintaining home and cars, and working with the boys' sports programs.

Merry Christmas and Warmest Regards,

Dave
(Don to some of you)
The Evans Family

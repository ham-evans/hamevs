Hi DeAun,

This is the note that I promised I would do after our telephone conversation today. The text is all being entered from dictation using a computer program called "Naturally Speaking." Please let me know if I missed any points from our phone call.

In your phone call today you recalled the day I was born on December 28, 1930.

Apparently it was early in the morning, when you were still in bed, when Mother went into labor. The doctor had arrived and was drinking coffee with Dad in the kitchen. Mother was on the couch/bed in the living room and was looking forward to receiving what was then called "gas," (laughing gas) to help ease the pain of childbirth.

However apparently I decided to come out into the world before anyone was ready so Mother did not have time to receive the promised "gas" before I was born. DeAun (7 years old) was supposed to be asleep in the bedroom, which she shared with Lenora (10 years old). DeAun heard me cry out, and reported this to Lenora, however Lenora said it was just a cat and to go back to sleep. DeAun knew better, so she used the "I need to go to the bathroom" excuse to find out what was going on. She recalls this day vividly and wanted to share with me.

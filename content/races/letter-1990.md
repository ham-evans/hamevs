Dear Family and Friends,

Just a short update on the Evans' happenings for 1990, as is our custom at Christmas time.

A happy event this year was the marriage of Tim to Karen Wedul, a delightful young lady from Minnesota. They were married on November 10th in her home town in northern Minnesota, Thief River Falls, in the north-western part of the state. As you might expect, only a small sprinkling of Tim's friends could make the long trip, so we are planning for a Spring reception for Tim and Karen here in California. They are making their home in Minneapolis, where they are both employed.

Bruce and Anita have just completed raising their financial support, and are now moving to Moscow, Idaho, where they will begin their Campus Crusade for Christ assignment at Washington State University. We are happy that they can now do what it is that they have been working for so long, however we will sorely miss their frequent visits from nearby San Diego.

Marily is having a good year attending Pasadena City College, working part time, and keeping up with her heavy schedule of socializing. She had a frustrating start this year, having to undergo a series of surgeries related to her shunt revision, but is now doing just fine. She hopes to transfer to UCLA in a year or so.

Nancy continues to be busy with the house and garden, and just keeping her family going. Her most enjoyable project has been the interior redecorating, which was essentially completed this year. We are now enjoying the new, western/Indian feel, which makes me feel right at home.

I continue to work long hours and do a considerable amount of travel associated with my job at JPL. My project, called "Mars Observer," is scheduled for launch during September, 1992, so we are heavily involved at this time with completing the development phase.

I hope this short note will help keep you posted on our family's activities. As always, we enjoy getting updated on your happenings as well. Have a joyous holiday season and a happy, prosperous new year.

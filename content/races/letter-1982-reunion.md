Dear Family,

As you may know from talking to either Lenora or myself by phone, Nancy and I would like to host a family reunion this summer. We've picked the afternoon/evening of August 13th as the time for everyone to be together at the same time, however you are all most welcome to drop by and visit with us and each other both before and after that day.

Since we have limited sleeping accommodations (both boys will be at home), I suggest you make reservations at a local hotel/motel, unless you want to combine your trip with a vacation stay at the beach or elsewhere. My suggestions:

Holiday Inn, 303 Cordova St., Pasadena, (213) 449-4000
Rates start at $75/night for two

Imperial 400 Motel, 1203 E. Colorado Blvd., Pasadena, (213) 449-3170
Rates start at $36/night for two

Early reservations are advised. Both of these are "Freeway-close" to La Canada, and have pools, etc. Unfortunately we don't have a nice pool like Gary's, but we do have a large grassy back yard for play.

For those of you that will be flying, I suggest you try to arrive at Burbank airport, if possible. We can easily meet your plane at Burbank. Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is undergoing a massive construction project to get ready for next year's Olympics, hence it is quite difficult to get in and out by car. If you do need to use LAX, I suggest you catch the Airport Limousine to Pasadena, and get off at the Hilton Hotel (last stop). We can easily pick you up there, and also provide local transportation for you.

The weather in Pasadena during August is typically warm-to-hot during the day and quite cool in the evenings, so bring sweaters. We would be happy to make reservations, get maps, etc. if you need help. Just let us know.

This note is going only to brothers and sisters only, however all members of the Clan are most welcome. Please invite your children/spouses/grandchildren for us. Hope you can all make it. Let us know your plans when they've been firmed up.

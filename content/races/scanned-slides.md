*Concise Index to Each Year*
*David Evans Family Slide Collection*
*December, 2008*

These were all slides that I took in addition to snapshots that are currently in albums, and also in addition to some home movies that will be converted to DVD format. They have been organized but not edited, and are listed by either the subject or the year (some years are skipped since I had apparently taken no slides in that period). Later photos are conventional prints in photo albums until later when we used a digital camera.

## The Air Force

Primary training at Spence air base in Moultrie Georgia. Next, basic training in jets in Laredo Air Force Base Texas and finally an assignment to the 474th Fighter Bomber Group, at Cannon Air Force Base, Clovis, New Mexico (where I was born!)

## 1960

Our honeymoon, the Apple Valley trip, Christmas and Herbold visit.

## 1961

4565 Viro Road, Canada, California. Rain in our flooded backyard, Christmas with Barry and Cynda.

## 1962

Riverside Hotel, visit to Putnams (Nancy's Sister and Family) in New York, and Christmas.

## 1963

Nancy when pregnant, a new baby Tim.

## 1964

Tim as a one year old.

## 1965

12 photos, mostly of trip to Balboa beach, with Bruce as a little guy.

## 1969

30 images. Fishing trip to Colorado, Easter, the Silverton train, overnight in Phoenix, and portraits taken at church.

## 1974

50 photos. Christmas with the Herbolds, Easter, gra-Y., Tim's birthday, San Diego trip to visit Nancy's cousins, Marily finally makes an appearance in slides.

## 1975 and 1977

Thulene's visit. Mostly photos of family and includes quite a few of Bruce's visit to Portland. Also Christmas with Patty and Ralph (Friends).

## 1978

Family activities, a Farmington reunion, Bruce's graduation from Junior High School, Brockmeyer's pool, and others.

## 1979 At Home

Trip to Boulder city, baseball, basketball, Spooky the dog and others.

## 1979 Early

Baseball, other backyard activities, including golf practice.

## 1979 N. West Vacation

100 images mostly of the vacation, including the Portland family reunion and the return trip.

## 1980

The Hayes family reunion in California, Bruce as an acolyte, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.

## 1980 Portraits

Five photos of portraits taken at church again.

## 1981

Tim's high school graduation, Nancy's birthday, and Halloween.

## 1981 Tim Track

About a dozen images of Tim taken during track meets.

## 1983

Visit to bay area (San Francisco) and Christmas.

## 1984

About a dozen images of the Olympics opening ceremony. We were guests of the Donald Lee's for this event.

## 1986

Our visit to Yellowstone Park including shots on the way and some of our RV.

## 1987

A trip to the Gold country of California, our trip to Hawaii.

## Cactus Road Home

A few images of our new home in Scottsdale.

## Family Reunions

About 80 images over the years, including a few of my Mother, Grace, several of our family, and several of my Brother or Sister's families of the Charles Evans' clan.

## General Hospital Softball

A few images that Marily wanted to keep when she watched the General Hospital program religiously.

## Snapshots Various

A variety of images that didn't seem to fit elsewhere or that I forgot to include. Includes La Canada basketball, girl scouts, and Christmas.

## Space Related

Several images that I wanted to keep from my experience with the space program. They include photos of the Mars Observer spacecraft and its launch as well.

## University of Colorado

About six photos that I wanted to keep of my two years at the University of Colorado. They include a couple from my instructing in light aircraft, such as a Piper Cub (and many others).

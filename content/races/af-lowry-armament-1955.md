## Transcription

### Ferry Order — December 3, 1954

Headquarters, 3415th Technical Training Wing (ATC), Lowry AFB, Colo.

2ndLt Evans, David D., AO 3011940, and 2ndLt Bass, Perkins B. III, AO 3010743, are placed on EDYTADFN 2 days WP on or about 3 Dec 54 to Malden AFB, MO, OTAIP, purpose to ferry T-6 back to Lowry AFB. Travel by military aircraft or government rail and/or bus authorized.

### Promotion to 1st Lieutenant — March 28, 1955

Headquarters, Technical Training Air Force (ATC), Gulfport, Mississippi. Special Orders Number 76.

DP announcement is made of the temporary promotion of the following named 2ndLt, Base indic, to the grade of 1stLt, USAF, UP Sec 515, Off Pers Act of 1947 with DR from date of this order.

Evans, David D., AO3011940 — Lowry AFB, Colo.

An automatic promotion after 18 months as a 2nd Lt.

### Graduation — Armament Systems Officer School, May 6, 1955

Headquarters, 3416th Student Squadron (Officer)(ATC), Lowry Air Force Base, Denver, Colorado. Personnel Actions Memorandum Number 43.

FOL PERS (ASO Pipeline) ATC this Unit are GRAD from Class 1711A ARMT SYS OFF CNS NR 083214, EFF 17 May 1955. Officers ASO DIR IY ASO IN ARMT OCC FLD until 16 November 1955 IAW AFR 36-78.

1stLt David D. Evans, AO3011940 is listed among the graduates, along with 1stLt Oscar E. Jones, 2ndLt James E. Leeth (his buddy Jim Leeth), and others.

### Assignment to Clovis AFB — April 27, 1955

Upon graduation from Armament Systems training, Evans is ordered to report to Clovis AFB as a duty station. He was keenly disappointed since Clovis, NM was "in the middle of nowhere" — and incidentally he had been born there 24 years earlier.

Dear Family and Friends,

It's Christmas time again...ready or not! Doesn't seem possible that another year has passed...

Just a short note this year to let you know that we are very well, indeed and hope that all of you are also. Needless to say we've all been very busy, but who hasn't?

Both Tim and Bruce are away at school, so we don't see very much of them. They are both active in the Campus Crusade for Christ program, and will be attending a Winter meeting during part of the Christmas vacation. We are pleased that they have chosen to participate in this activity. Tim is a senior at Univ. of Calif.-Irvine this year, and he hopes to serve with the Campus Crusade organization when he graduates, hopefully with an assignment at a College or University. He'll need your prayers, since he will have quite a challenge to obtain financial support. Bruce is trying to line up a job this summer, hopefully in the Airline industry since that is his professional interest.

Nancy, Marily, and I had a really fun vacation this year. We rented a mobile home and traveled to Yellowstone and the California Gold country for a wonderful two weeks. It was quite an experience, since we hadn't done that sort of thing before. We are now looking forward to an East Coast historical tour vacation this next summer. We may rent a mobile home then as well.

I had an enjoyable time at my Farmington High School 35th reunion this past summer. It was the Class of '49's first reunion, and was a real joy to see and visit with some of my dearest friends. We even got to visit with a few of our former teachers; that was also a real pleasure. I was surprised that a few of my classmates have already retired. It will be a few years before we can seriously consider this, but the day will come....

Meanwhile we do our best to enjoy, and hope that you do also.

Our best,

Don & Nancy

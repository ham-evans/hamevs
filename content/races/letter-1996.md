Dear Family and Friends,

This season gives us a chance to reflect on the past year as well as give thanks for our blessings, which very much includes staying in touch with each of you.

Our second year of retirement in Scottsdale has proven to be just as enjoyable as the first. We keep fully occupied doing a variety of things, focused on enjoying our extended family and pursuing our hobby of Native American art and lore that is such a part of the Southwest.

We had some very nice visits with Bruce and grandchildren in Orlando. This year a new member of the family arrived with Jamison on August 5th, joining Tyler (4) and Kenzie (3). On our last trip, during early November, we visited Cape Canaveral for the launch of the Mars Global Surveyor. (Dave was heavily involved in the genesis of this mission while he was at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory.) It was too windy to accept the final pre-launch briefing, and so we watched from a park along with several of the area locals and their lawn chairs, at a distance of about 5 miles.

We also attended the family reunion this year.

Dave has been involved in consulting with a group at the University of Arizona, located in Tucson, that is proposing a series of scientific experiments on a small spacecraft that would be beneficial at the two-way point between the earth and the sun. He also still enjoys his computing hobby (but he always needs a new toy for the computer!).

We see Tim and Karen, and daughter Katie (1) quite often since they only live twenty-some minutes away. It has been a real pleasure of parenthood to see how Tim has evolved into a very good and caring family man. Tim has been quite busy as a third-year associate at the Phoenix office of Squire, Sanders, & Dempsey, an international law firm.

Bruce has accepted a position with the University of Idaho, where he will coordinate the University's Summer and Extended Learning programs. The family is excited to be moving back to Moscow, Idaho, where they had lived before moving to Orlando. The family, understandably, will miss many aspects of the Campus Crusade program and those associates. Anita is getting ready; they will be arriving at about Christmas time. She and the children will visit us in Scottsdale and her folks in California while Bruce drives to Moscow.

Marily has moved to Phoenix and has an apartment located only a few minutes from us. We are delighted to have her close to us. She is starting her own business as a make-up artist, specializing in corporate services and related work. She enjoys daily workouts at a local fitness center as well as baby-sitting Katie Rose on occasion.

We wish you and yours good health, Merry Christmas, and a Happy New Year. Please stay in touch!

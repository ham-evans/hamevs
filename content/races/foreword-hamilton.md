As I've gotten older, I've come to appreciate the traits passed on to me by Grandave more and more. Love of chemistry, science, space, adventure, among many others. Regrettably, I don't think I realized how much we had shared interests until after he passed away.

For some reason, Grandave left me his stories, photos, and much more. I've sat on it for way too long, and have finally decided to put it to good use and share it with the people he would have wanted to read it.

As a kid all adults are adults. But it's clear to me now that Grandave lived a pretty special and unique life. Although I wish this story was 300 pages long as it easily could have been, I hope you appreciate as I have a glimpse into the life that Grandave lived told in his own words.

There's already one of his great grandchildren who bears his name and I'm sure there will be more.

Hope you enjoy.

*Hamilton*

Dear Family & Friends,

Here it is almost Christmas again, and we aren't at all ready. Thought we would give you a wee bit of news from our place, in hopes that you will also do so from your place.

We've had a busy, interesting year, the highlight of which was a delightful couple of days when the Evans' held their family reunion at our place in mid-August. We spent considerable time painting and polishing, which was long overdue anyway, and enjoyed Dave's brothers and sisters along with a few spouses and children. Reunions are a long-standing custom which they try to do every 2 to 4 years.

Tim is now a Junior at the University of California — Irvine, located about 60 miles east of Pasadena. He is taking his degree work in Economics, but his real interest is in the Campus Crusade for Christ program, a Christian study/outreach activity. This past summer he participated in a Crusade project at San Diego, working full time at a local hotel and spending evenings and weekends in organized activities. He is currently actively involved in organizing the UC Irvine group participation in a major young peoples' Christian conference to be held right after Christmas in Kansas City.

Bruce is a Sophomore at San Jose State University at San Jose, California (South of San Francisco). He is enrolled in an Aeronautics option, and hopes to either fly or become involved in the business/operations aspect of commercial aviation. Bruce is also active in the Campus Crusade program, and plans to attend the Kansas City conference as well.

Marily is in 7th grade, and is fully immersed in many of the typical activities of the Jr. High age group. This includes plenty of homework, rock music, the school choir, boys, long phone conversations, etc.

We are barely able to keep up with all of the above. During our spare time we both enjoy using the computer, Nancy for word processing and school/skills purposes, and Dave for exploring and programming. Dave is a program manager at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, involved in applying their planetary spacecraft technology to the needs of the Air Force. He enjoys it, partly because he served a stint in the Air Force many years ago.

We've had a good year, and hope you have also. Please keep in touch with a note or call occasionally. We wish you the best for 1984.

Our Love,

Dave & Nancy Evans
Tim, Bruce, and Marily

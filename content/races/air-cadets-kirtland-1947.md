*Farmington, San Juan County, New Mexico — Friday, September 12, 1947*

![Farmington Air Cadets at Kirtland Field](/races/air-cadets-kirtland-1947.jpg)

The above six Cadets represented the Town of Farmington at the Civil Air Patrol Encampment at Kirtland Field, in Albuquerque this summer. Cadets from all over the State meet for two weeks every summer to study Military Aircraft on the ground and actual Operation in Flight.

The Farmington cadets are, reading left to right: **R. L. Kysar, J. D. Hurst, C. W. Lester, D. W. Martin, D. D. Evans, P. H. Bingel.** (A. P. Photo.)

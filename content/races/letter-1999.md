We are still very much enjoying Scottsdale and retirement. This has been a busy year for us... guess that is o.k. since they say it keeps you young. I am still heavily involved in volunteer work for the AARP Tax-Aide program, which does computer-prepared tax returns for low to moderate income people (with an emphasis on serving seniors). Although tax season is only 3 months long we are involved for at least 6 months in preparation and the like. I also serve as president of the condominium association where Marily owns a condo, and that has been quite a challenge recently.

Marily has moved to Nashville where she has some good friends in the music industry. We helped move her in June, involving the usual myriad of things associated with relocation. It gave us the opportunity to convoy across country and spend some time in an entirely different, and interesting, city. She is doing fine there, working as a receptionist at an upscale hairdressing salon. However, she isn't too happy with the cold winter weather in Nashville, recognizing that she has lived in either California or Arizona all her life.

Bruce and Anita and family of four are doing well in Moscow, Idaho. Bruce has an interesting job with a specialty software firm, applying the computer expertise that he learned while at the University of Idaho. Anita works part time as an occupational therapist at a local medical facility. We visited them in August and expect to see them here in Scottsdale for a week in February. Tyler (8), Kenzie (6), Jamison (3) are looking forward to Christmas and the visit to Scottsdale. Preston (1) just enjoys doing whatever the big kids do.

Tim and Karen and family are also doing well, living only a couple of miles from our former home in La Canada, California. Tim is employed by a major law firm in Los Angeles and Karen keeps fully occupied with Katie (4) and Hamilton (2) as well as some tutoring. We are able to see them frequently since they are only a 6-hour drive away.

This year we are celebrating our 40th wedding anniversary by arranging for all of the above to spend a few days with us around February 20th. It will be a treat to have them all here at the same time.

Our best wishes to you during holiday season and a happy year 2000!

<p style="margin-bottom:0.25rem"><em>La Canada, California</em></p>
<p style="margin-top:0"><em>December 15, 1965</em></p>

Dear Family,

Well, we are finally getting out a family letter this year after trying to do so for the past two years. We are all fine. Nancy keeps busy continuously (at least) keeping up the house, and keeping up with the boys. I am still with the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, and now the Group Leader of the Combustion Device Group (rocket engines and propellants).

We had an enjoyable vacation at the beach this summer. Our one-week stay in a rented house on Balboa Island was a welcome change from our usual routine. We spent most of every day on the beach, playing in the sand and water or just watching the boats go by. Tim got a real charge out of going on the ferry boat and eating frozen bananas, a local delicacy (a frozen banana dipped in chocolate and rolled in nuts or chocolate bits).

Shortly after we returned we were delighted to have mother visit for a couple of weeks. She had intended to stay but one week, however she felt fine and we convinced her to stay a while longer. One weekend we even got her out to Disneyland for a few hours. Tim still talks about riding on the train with Grandmother Grace, and eating outside on a table with an umbrella over it.

Tim is approaching the "I can do it myself" stage and exerts his independence in many ways. For example, he insists upon putting on his own trousers, label-in-the-back and all, except that both legs wind up in the same pants leg. He spends a lot of time playing with his blocks and "reading" or coloring, and is quite advanced for his age, of course. Bruce, at 16 months is in to everything. He delights in knocking down Tim's block-houses and in rough-housing, and is on an awful basis.

In recent weeks we have been trying to decide what to do about future housing plans. We have just about outgrown our 2 Bdrm & Den, 1 bath house, and would like to either remodel or relocate such that we could have a 3 Bdrm, 2 bath, family-room situation. As usual, the things you really like are also expensive, so we are still searching. Perhaps by next Christmas we will have this resolved.

We are looking forward to a quiet holiday with two small tree-climbers to observe and photograph. Tim is impressed with Santa and is expecting great things of him. He started out asking for "a semi-trailer cement mixer and a real purple motorcycle" until we told him he really needed to think it over. Since the slip-leader he changed his order to "blocks and a new bathrobe." I think Santa might compromise his order. Bruce is right on the edge of enjoying his toys and will be quite easy. Tim goes to bed early with the decorations and all of Tim's toys. Nancy is hopeful that she will receive a new refrigerator to replace our "temporary" used one of four vintages that we bought at a renter's price. "I just want something simple—like a freezer—but I'll settle for something less—like a power-lender."

Our best wishes to you for a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year!

<p style="margin-bottom:0.25rem"><em>Love,</em></p>
<p style="margin-top:0"><em>Nancy & Don</em></p>

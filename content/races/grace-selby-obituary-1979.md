*Farmington Daily Sun, Sunday, January 14, 1979*

![Grace Evans Selby obituary and photo](/races/grace-selby-obituary-1979.jpg)

## Former County Resident Dies

Grace Evans Selby, a long-time resident of San Juan County, died Friday in Las Vegas, Nevada, at the age of 79. Mrs. Selby came to Farmington in 1934 with her husband, Charles Hamilton Evans, and their three children.

Evans had first arrived in Farmington in 1923, moved away, and returned with the family. Four more children were born and remained here. The family belonged to the First Methodist Church.

Mrs. Selby was preceded in death by Evans who died Dec. 25, 1940. Survivors include a daughter, Mrs. Robert F. Brothers, and son, Carrol M. Evans, both of Farmington, two other daughters, two other sons, 16 grandchildren, 17 great-grandchildren, two sisters, and a brother. One son and one brother are deceased.

Funeral services will be held at 1:30 p.m. Monday at the First Methodist Church, the Rev. Bob Harrison officiating. Burial will be in Greenlawn Cemetery.

## Obituary

SELBY — Church service for Grace Evans Selby, 79, 1239 N. Butler, will be held at 1:30 p.m. Monday at the First Methodist Church, the Rev. Bob Harrison officiating. Burial will be in Greenlawn Cemetery. Mrs. Selby died Friday in Las Vegas, Nevada. She was born on June 23, 1899, in Cookville, Texas, the daughter of David Calvin Myers and Jeffie Anna Cook, and married Charles Hamilton Evans on Feb. 22, 1920, in Memphis, Texas. Evans preceded her in death on December 25, 1940. She was a housewife and resident of New Mexico for 45 years. Survivors include three daughters; Mrs. Robert F. Brothers of Farmington, Mrs. George M. Lancaster of Weslaco, Texas, Mrs. David A. Walsdorf Jr., Jefferson Parish, La.; three sons; Carrol M. Evans of Farmington, David D. Evans of La Canada Flintridge, Calif., Gary L. Evans of Boulder City, Nev.; two sisters: Mrs. W.R. Goodrum and Mrs. Ralph R. Loyd; one brother: Barney Myers of Belin. There are 16 grandchildren and 17 great-grandchildren. A son, Charles H. Evans Jr. of Denver and a brother, Ordway Campbell, Meyer, Denver, preceded her in death. Pallbearers are Richard Brothers, Charles Brothers, Ray Harvath, Bobby Williams, Dale Harvath, Byron Evans and Ronald Evans. Arrangements are under the direction of Brewer Funeral Home.

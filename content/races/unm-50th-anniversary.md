## 50th Anniversary — Class of 1953 Reunion Book

![Class of 1953 Reunion Book entry](/races/unm-50th-p1.jpg)

Donald Evans (at UNM); I now use David D Evans

Spouse: Nancy Evans

Since 1953, I:

- Entered the US Air Force and became a Jet Fighter Pilot
- Returned to School at the University of Colorado receiving a BS in Aeronautical Engineering
- Accepted a position as a Research Engineer at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Pasadena, CA, conducting research on liquid propellants and rocket engines for spacecraft
- Married Nancy and raised three children; now have 7 Grandchildren
- Participated in the exploration of all of the planets (except Pluto), becoming the manager of a Mars project
- Retired as the Deputy Manager of Planetary Flight Projects in 1994
- Moved to Scottsdale Arizona, where we presently live

Currently in my spare time I:

- Enjoy Grandchildren and Native American art work
- Enjoy digital photography and computers
- Am documenting my experiences for my heirs
- Serve as the State Technical Specialist for the AARP TaxAide program, which provides free income tax preparation to seniors and lower income folks

While at UNM I remember:

- Panty raids, and the police quickly retreating after some fireworks were set off
- Air Force ROTC, with Cadets marching in Review (Korean war was underway)
- Football games, with Vodka-loaded ripe oranges
- Not being served at a local restaurant because we were with two black students (this was a test case; we declared a student boycott of the restaurant)
- Trying to stay awake after working all night
- Studying until very late hours in order to get the highest score in the organic chemistry class
- Good friends, good times, hard work, not enough money, etc, etc.

Words of Wisdom: A good reputation takes long and hard work; don't lose it!

## Class of 1953 — Commencement Questionnaire

![Class of 1953 Commencement Questionnaire](/races/unm-50th-p2.jpg)

David D Evans (aka Donald Evans during that era)

Spouse: Nancy Evans

Received BS degree, with honors in Chemistry

Chemistry

Guido Daub (who has since passed away)

I remember (not in any particular order):

- Panty raids, and the police quickly retreating back into their van after some fireworks were set off,
- Air Force ROTC, with the Cadets marching in Review (this was during the Korean war),
- Football games, with Vodka-loaded ripe oranges,
- Not being served by a local restaurant because we were with two black students (this was a test case: we declared a student boycott of the restaurant subsequently),
- Dorm food: acceptable; Nearby Mexican restaurant: excellent!
- Trying to stay awake in class after working all night,
- Studying until very late hours in order to get the highest score in the organic chemistry class,
- Good friends, good times, not enough money, etc., etc.

Now:

I have retired from a career that involved serving in the Air Force as a jet fighter pilot, and as an engineer/manager participating in the robotic exploration of the planets while at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Pasadena, California. I also went back to school for an additional degree (at the University of Colorado).

I stay active with hobbies, grandchildren, and am heavily involved in volunteer work with the AARP Taxaide program, where I am the State technology specialist.

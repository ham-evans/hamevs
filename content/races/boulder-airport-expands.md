**Boulder Airport Expands Services; $100,000 Improvement Starts Soon**

**Runway Paving Is Main Feature Of Bond Program**

A $100,000 improvement project at the Boulder municipal airport is scheduled to start in June. The work will be done under a master plan providing for further development in the future.

The master plan, after being revised to meet recommendations of the Federal Civil Aeronautical Administration, is back in the hands of the CAA for final approval, expected about the first of next month.

The main feature of this year's project will be the paving of the east-west runway. Its course will be changed slightly but it will remain at its present length, 4,200 feet, and width, 75 feet. The master plan calls for a 5,700-foot runway ultimately.

A small administration building will be constructed at the airport.

**Staff Is Enlarged; Flight School Wins Double Approval**

Service at Boulder municipal airport was expanded considerably during the past year, and further expansion is planned as new construction gets underway on the landing strip and buildings.

Lazy 8 Aviation Inc., which operates the airport under contract with the city, plans privately to start building new shop and hangar facilities when the $100,000 city-federal improvement project starts. An administration building is planned as a part of that program.

Mr. and Mrs. Miles Holub and Mr. and Mrs. Bob Jones are incorporators of Lazy 8 Aviation. During the past year they added five new staff members: Chuck Miller and **Dave Evans**, flight instructors; Ted Atkinson and Ennis Huckabay, aircraft mechanics; and Clifford Brock, mechanic's helper.

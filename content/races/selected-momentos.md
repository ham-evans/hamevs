## 1969 — Letter from Mom (Grace)

![Handwritten letter from Grace, 1969 — page 1](/races/momentos-04.jpg)

![Handwritten letter from Grace, 1969 — page 2](/races/momentos-05.jpg)

1969

Thursday A.M.

Dear Don, Nancy, Tim & Bruce,

Thanks for the paper. I enjoyed reading and looking at all the pretty floats. They were beautiful.

I am enjoying my pretty light. I leave it on the T.V. It is real pretty & attractive. I put the blue color in it so you would know, I have had a lot of compliments on it.

Tim, thanks for the pretty picture frame. Guess you would call it that, it is real pretty. Cody sure likes to look through it.

Bruce, the little Christmas ornament you made is real putty. My, put it on the tree. Gary thought it was so pretty he wanted to put it on the tree. He said Bruce made it.

Tim, I hope you and Bruce are O.K. and like your school.

Cecelia, Charlie & Cody have moved to Albuquerque. I think I told you, he has a good position selling insurance to college students. They just moved and are here for a few days.

Dickie & Ann will be here in a little over a week on their way to Las Vegas for here. Dickie's wife wrote they are anxious to come since they haven't been here since their marriage. He was transferred there.

I am sending some clippings thought you would enjoy.

Mom

Be good to Delta and I am sure you are.

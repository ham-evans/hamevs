<p style="text-align:center;margin-top:0">This letter describes an extensive trip I took in my Mooney Mite airplane.</p>

Hello again:

Enjoyed my vacation thoroughly and wish to thank all involved for their fine hospitality. Thought you might be interested in a short summary of the trip.

**September 4:** Departed San Fernando airport and proceeded to Las Vegas. Flight time two hours. Fine weather except pretty bumpy over the desert. Visited with Gary for a while although he had to work that night.

**September 5:** To Farmington by way of Lake Mead, Grand Canyon and the Painted Desert. 3:20, clear and bumpy. (I actually flew just below the Grand Canyon rim.) Stopped and refueled at Tuba City airport. Then to Farmington. (Shiprock beckons from many, many miles away.) The Robert Brothers' and Carroll Evans' met me at the airport. All ate at Carroll's that evening. Visited with many friends the following day. Farmington is still growing!

**September 7:** To Albuquerque, 1:40, nice weather and smooth air. Ate out at the Hacienda in old town that night. Good Mexican food and good company courtesy of the Goodrums and the Lloyds. Visited with some friends there including one of my chemistry professors, Dr. Daub. Albuquerque is still growing.

**September 9:** To Boulder, Colorado after stopping in Trinidad to refuel. 3:40 + 2:00 hours, weather clear but very rough strong headwinds and extremely bumpy. Met by Cadillac courtesy of Lazy-8 Aviation, my former employer while attending school at Colorado University. (This is where I instructed in light airplanes while I was a student.) Stayed with Ms. Norma Immell, my former landlady, who treated us boys as her own sons. Visited with my former roommate, Gobel James, who was still in school at CU. We took the Trail Ridge Drive through Rocky Mountain National Park in Gobel's convertible. Certainly was refreshing to breathe clean air again. Visited with Charles and Dottie in Denver on Saturday. (Charles H Evans was my brother.) We drove over and visited LenaBell and Grandmother Sloan, who is not doing very well now. Charles and Dottie are proud owners of a new Thunderbird and it is a beauty.

**September 13:** Started the trip back to Pasadena. First to Las Vegas by way of over the Rockies and with a stop at Grand Junction for refueling, 2:05 + 5:00, weather good until over Utah where there were a few scattered showers. Headwinds all the way. Saw Gary again after locating his new residence. It was his night off this time so we had a nice visit. (As an aside, I became temporarily uncertain of my position — nice words for sort of lost — between Grand Junction and Las Vegas, hence the 5 hour flight, landing with low fuel.)

**September 14:** To San Fernando airport, 2:40, still high clouds.

---

**Total flying time:** 23:30 hours
**Total equivalent road miles:** 2,662
**Total direct operating cost:** $40 for fuel
*(Less than two cents per mile)*

Again, thanks for everything. At least I have had enough flying for a week or so. By the way, how about another family reunion soon?

Following is a sample of the flight plans that I used. I used a small clipboard on my knee so I could easily refer to these in flight. The Mooney had only a compass for navigation, and the only radio aid was a set that was used prior to landing at some of the airports. (ie, no direction finders nor GPS, nor autopilot.) To check the fuel you looked behind yourself at a glass sight gauge since the fuel tank was directly behind the pilot.

## Transcription

### Assignment to 474th Fighter-Bomber Wing — June 8, 1955

Headquarters, 312th Fighter Bomber Wing (TAC), Clovis Air Force Base, Clovis, New Mexico. Special Orders Number 119.

1st Lt David D. Evans, AO3011940 is relieved from assignment with the 312th Fighter-Bomber Wing and assigned to the 474th Fighter-Bomber Group. Orders detail various personnel moves including Evans.

ESVMO VOCOMDR 26 May 55 is eff as fols: 1st Lt David D. Evans AO3011940 is rel fr asgt w/Hq 312th FTRBOMGR (TAC) this staj; asgd L/412TH FTRBOMGR (TAC) this staj.

### Appointed Group Armament Systems Officer — July 7, 1955

Headquarters, 474th Fighter Bomber Group, Clovis Air Force Base, Clovis, New Mexico. Personnel Actions Memorandum Number 191.

1st Lt David D. Evans, AO 3 011 940, this hq, this sta, is asgd Primarily as Op Asst Armn Sys Officer (AFSC 3231). Func Acct Code: 30010.

Also announced: AFQT score results for airmen at the station, showing various scores and qualifications.

### Assigned as Group Armament Officer — September 28, 1955

Headquarters, 474th Fighter-Bomber Group. Personnel Actions Memorandum Number 27.

1st Lt David D. Evans, AO 3011940, this hq, this sta, AFSC 3231, ADD AFSC 4641, is relieved Primarily 3231 (Group Armament Officer) and asgd Primly 4641 (Group Materiel Officer). No change in functional account code. Vice Major Russell P. Kenney.

### Release from Active Duty — September 12, 1956

This set of orders released Evans from active duty and assigned him to the Air Force reserve. This is when he went back to school at the University of Colorado, with veteran benefits (tuition and books).

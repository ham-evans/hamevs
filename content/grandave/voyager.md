*December 3, 2017*

*Read the below article first. The article is a bit long. I am pleased, however.*

*I was the responsible propulsion engineer for Voyager during the development and early implementation phase. I successfully proposed the use of hydrazine propellent and the mechanization of the system with a full redundancy and shared reserves with the launch system upper stage. This was in 1973 in a presentation to management. This shared reserves approach enabled attitude control for the extended mission, which is now gone 40 years.*

*For those with some chemistry hydrazine is N2H4. It decomposes over a catalyst to NH3 and H2. The thrusters involved are small of about 3 oz of thrust in 20 msec pulses in attitude control mode. (Try doing a tiny puff with your hand a few inches from your face.) They were a devil to develop. But that's another story.*

*— Grandave*

<p style="margin-bottom:0.25rem"><em>La Canada, California</em></p>
<p style="margin-top:0"><em>December 14, 1962</em></p>

Dear Family,

We said last year that a family letter would follow soon after our Christmas cards. Well, here it is! It is nearing two time four years as one goes along. No big news from last year was that we had bought a house, and we have been spending most of this year messing with it with some visible results and some improvements known only to us.

Our big item this year, of course, is that we are having an addition to the family. Nancy is feeling pretty well and is beginning to get excited and getting more and more excited as the time draws nearer. The prospect of getting being more active in the work of baby, but everything is fine so far. He will continue to the most elaborate place he is born. We've been busy preparing the nursery (installation floors, painting, etc.) and have enjoyed walking through the baby section looking for furnishings and the like. At any rate, a major change in our way of life lies ahead and we will, I'm equally, greatly adjust to course.

This summer I had to take a business trip to Washington, D.C., so we took advantage of the situation to incorporate a short vacation. We spent a week, part of it on business, on the east coast. One of the times was spent visiting Nancy's sister in her new place, that I had never met. We had a very enjoyable, although somewhat hectic, vacation. The trip back was via airliner, passing directly over Farmington, but I fully intended to visit Farmington by auto during the fall, however our plans have been somewhat disrupted by the baby.

I am still working at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory in Pasadena. My job involves the evaluation of new rocket propellants. This includes the determination of physical and chemical properties, handling procedures, the firing of small rocket engines with the various combinations, and similar duties. We are all quite proud of the Mariner experiment, which came so close. Two vehicles are scheduled for launching next summer. I spent about two years working on the propellant which was used to make the mid-course direction change to put the spaceship on direct aim of the vehicle. I am presently working on an improved version of this fuel which we hope to use in future spacecraft of this type.

Nancy plans to continue with her part-time work. She has had all sorts of ideas to quit her job since she has been at JPL for about seven years and does enjoy her work and her many friends at work. Both she and I are looking forward to the next big change coming — also the trip! It is quite a chore to accomplish a season's worth of household duties during the weekends. We graduated that the weekends are for that. Three years, however, since it allowed us to buy the house and otherwise get set up. Tim seems to be coming around just right, and we both feel it will still be able to keep ahead of things, freeing me for more important duties, such as puttering and the like.

<p style="margin-bottom:0.25rem"><em>Love,</em></p>
<p style="margin-top:0"><em>Nancy & Don</em></p>

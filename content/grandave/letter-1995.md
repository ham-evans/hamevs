Dear Family and Friends,

Just a short Christmas update; we really do enjoy retirement and Scottsdale, Arizona!

We moved to Scottsdale in mid-January, and are settled into our new home. We have been very busy getting organized in our new environment and taking advantage of every opportunity to go/see/do many things that interest us in Arizona, and elsewhere. We especially enjoy the Native American art and lore that is such a part of this locality. We also are managing to spend a fair amount of time with our children and their families, with the emphasis on our Grandchildren, of course. As a bonus, we get to see more of our family that is here and our friends that are located in this area. Tim and family are located in Phoenix, and my nephew, Dick Brothers, and family are located only a few minutes away, which allows us to also see more of my Sister, Lenora, who visits them from time to time. We all, siblings and Evans family cousins, got together this summer in Southern Colorado. For the first time in several years we were all able to attend over a week end, including my sister, Lee Ella, who had been unable to attend for some time. We had a great time, sharing early family memories, especially as remembered by my three sisters (who are a bit older, and have a much better memory than I!).

We are able to see Tim and Karen quite a bit, since they live only 30 minutes away. We expect to see even more of them in the future. Karen just gave birth on December 1st to Katherine Julia Grace Evans. Mother, Baby, and Katie are doing fine! (Katie is the name of her Grandmother on Karen's side, and Grace is my mother's name.) Tim is keeping pretty busy as a second-year associate at the Phoenix office of Squire, Sanders and Dempsey, an international law firm. Karen taught sixth grade at Kachina Country Day School until the Fall semester, when Katie was about due.

We were able to have two nice visits with Bruce and Anita, along with Tyler (3 1/2), and Kenzie (2). Marily, Nancy, and I flew to Orlando in April for a visit with them in their new home. Also, they spent several weeks in Ft. Collins, Colorado at Campus Crusade's summer program, and we had a delightful two weeks with them there, during Phoenix's most severe hot weather, as it turned out! We are looking forward to their visit here during the Christmas season.

Marily completed her TV and Movie professional make-up course in Hollywood this year. She is anticipating moving to Phoenix in January, and getting in on the recent demand for people in her field to support the expansion of studios in the Phoenix area. She has always had her good friends and extended families that she developed as a result of baby sitting. We are looking forward to having her here, especially to enjoy her role as an Aunt for Katie.

This Christmas is going to be a special one for our family. For the first time in many years, we will share Christmas together at our home. This is a real treat, especially with Grandchildren to enjoy.

We wish you and yours good health, Merry Christmas, and Happy new year. Our phone number in Scottsdale is (602) 991-1447. Please stay in touch!

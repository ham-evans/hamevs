Hi Family,

I'm doing just a short update this year. We are all fine and busy as usual with volunteer work for AARP and with Family doings, all of which we enjoy. A highlight of our year was the celebration of our 40th wedding anniversary. It was special because we were able to get our extended family together for a few days, something we hadn't been able to accomplish for several years.

Marily moved back from Nashville this summer and is enrolled in class to become licensed as a Massage Therapist. She likes it very much, and we do too, especially since she practices on us! Needless to say we are happy to have her located nearby in her Condominium.

Bruce and Anita (Moscow, ID) are busy with their four young ones, with their work (Anita part-time), and with their Church activities. We visited them this summer, and are likely to do so again before too long. After all, that is part of the joy of being a Grandparent!

Tim and Karen welcomed another son, Sebastian, on October 30th. They purchased a classic older South Pasadena home this year and are enjoying restoring it to its original state (which will take a while, since it had been neglected for many years). Fortunately we are able to visit them regularly since Southern California is only a 6-hour drive from Scottsdale.

Our best wishes to you during the holiday season and a happy and healthy new year.

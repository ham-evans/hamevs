## Transcription

Six USAA (United Services Automobile Association) insurance statements showing Dave's addresses as he moved from the Air Force through graduate school to JPL and married life:

**1. 1956 — Clovis Air Force Base, New Mexico**
David D. Evans, Lt USAF, 474th Ftr Bmr Gp HQ, Clovis AFB, N. Mex. Member #328838. Renewal premium and savings. *Paid by check, 9 Aug '56.*

**2. August 1957 — Boulder, Colorado**
David D. Evans, Lt AFRES, 1507 Bluebell St, Boulder, Colo. Attending the University of Colorado for his aeronautical engineering degree. Savings: $16.60. Policy premium: $62.88. *Paid by check, 8/10/57.*

**3. August 1958 — Pasadena, California**
David D. Evans, Lt USAFR, 290 N Euclid 307, Pasadena, Calif. Just graduated from CU and started at JPL. Savings: $12.70. Policy premium: $95.04. Total due: $82.34.

**4. August 1959 — Sunland, California**
David D. Evans, Lt AFRES, 10424 Parr Ave Apt 2B, Sunland, Calif. The apartment he shared with high school buddy George Smith, as mentioned in the Mooney Mite trip letter. Savings: $22.40. Policy premium: $107.58. Total due: $85.18. *Paid by check, 8/31/59.*

**5. August 1960 — Glendale, California**
David D. Evans, Lt AFRES, 3501 B Stancrest Dr, Glendale 9, Calif. Savings: $25.30. Policy premium: $37.30. *Paid by check, 8/5/60.*

**6. April 1961 — La Canada, California**
David D. Evans, Lt AFRES, 4565 Viro Rd, La Canada, Calif. Their first home — purchased with Nancy in April 1961. Policy premiums: $40.15 and $15.00. Payment: $53.20. Balance circled: $1.80. *Paid.*

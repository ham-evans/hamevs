Dear Family and Friends,

Hope this note finds you all ready and in good health. Have meant to call each of you before now, and will try to do so sometime over the holidays period.

We are all fine, awfully busy it seems, but that's usually the case for all of us. I have been doing some traveling to Washington, DC recently on business, and am kind of traveling at an expert-level (so far I have it figured so it takes only one suit). Though I still do a little work on my computer, but use it mostly now for Christmas cards to those on my list.

Nancy has been spending a lot of time visiting her mother, Delta, and has also been doing some volunteer work at the local convalescent home several blocks away during May, and it soon on a pleasant pace. I'm doing Marily's mum on occasion since she keeps the household going nicely and generally heading our household gang.

Marily is now a Freshman (Frosh-person) at La Canada High School, where she also sings with the "Spartan Singers." She is in the gifted program for English, and other typical subjects. She is a good student, and enjoys school, but not the homework of course!

As many of you know, Tim graduated from the University of California, Irvine, last June, and is a full-time staff member in Campus Crusade for Christ International. He worked awfully hard to raise his financial support over the summer and fall, and has just reported to Michigan State University, where he will begin his assignment. He is excited and much appreciates the contributions. He is really on his support team, the participation of some of our family and friends.

Bruce is a Senior at San Jose State University, majoring in Aeronautics. He spent last summer working in the San Jose area, and is also active in his local Campus Crusade program. We are looking forward to a nice visit with him over his Semester break during late December and January, during his senior year.

Our Love, Dave & Nancy Evans & Family

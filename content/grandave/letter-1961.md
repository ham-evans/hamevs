<p style="margin-bottom:0.25rem"><em>La Canada, California</em></p>
<p style="margin-top:0"><em>January, 1961</em></p>

Dear Family and Friends,

Since it is the end of the year, we decided that it was now or never to get out a family letter. Hope that you all had a prosperous and enjoyable year. Our first year together has certainly been wonderful.

We especially enjoyed the family reunion and Nancy was grateful for the early opportunity to meet all of my family. We are only sorry that Charles couldn't make it.

The rest of the year since the reunion has been fairly eventful for us. We have been trying to take advantage of our present situation, i.e., no children, by going places and doing things as much as the budget allows. This has included such events as attending performances at the Hollywood Bowl and seeing the road show version of the "Music Man." During October I attended a rocket propellant conference in San Diego for three days and Nancy & I drove around a lot of attractions to accompany me and turn in the nights. Shortly after this trip we accompanied my lodge crowd on the nights of the Los Angeles Dodgers' TV Festival, to Disneyland, by chartered bus. We spent Thanksgiving with Mother. We spent most of the time visiting with Mother and Bill. Tim tried to be an attraction to Mother alone, since he was not doing very well at the time. He purchased a second-hand old blue-and-white Buick for $50. Nancy has had quite difficulty caring for him at that time. He has apparently improved somewhat since, although both financially and physically he has quite a way to go — at times so I urge you to write her often and give her your support. It is a very difficult thing for her to be left alone with just one or two. We must make the best of things as they are.

Nancy and I spent a week or so during December preparing for a Christmas party which we gave for 16 friends from work. We had a lot of fun in giving the party and especially enjoyed constructing a 8 ft plywood Christmas chimney with Santa's legs protruding. I owe you pictures of the character some day. To report Christmas Day, two of my friends from work, James Stallcup, came for dinner with us, as did Col. James Berthold, is stationed. The Berholds have two daughters. We all had turkey and all the trimmings. We also enjoyed a very fine dinner with Jim's brothers and families then near Long Beach.

Last of all I succeeded in selling the little airplane on the 1st of January, 45 days to spare with it (and a single-seated airplane isn't of much use when one is married).

Our best wishes for a happy new year to you all. Hope to hear from you guys occasionally and hope to write to you a little more often.

<p style="margin-bottom:0.25rem"><em>Lots of Love,</em></p>
<p style="margin-top:0"><em>Nancy & Don</em></p>

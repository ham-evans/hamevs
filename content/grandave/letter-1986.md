Dear Dorothy,

It seems like just yesterday that we were all together at the picnic in Farmington. We really enjoyed the reunion and look forward to the next one in a couple of years. Thanks to Lenora and the C. M. Evans' for all their hard work.

We are all busy as usual, and are doing just fine. Since we had a nice visit this summer, I'll just recap briefly here what's going on now.

As a Sophomore in high school, Marily is studying hard, and looking forward to learning how to drive. She was just accepted into the National Honor Society, for which we are justifiably proud. She also is a tried and true soap opera addict (especially General Hospital), and is a member of the drama club.

Bruce is in his last year in the Aeronautics program at San Jose State University, and is serving as a Resident Associate in one of the Dormitories (for room and board). We are looking forward to his Semester break, beginning just before Christmas. He currently plans to work for a year or two in the San Jose area, and then join the Staff of Campus Crusade for Christ, similar to what Tim is doing.

Tim's assignment is still at Michigan State University, in East Lansing. He is completing two years there, and most probably will be reassigned this summer. We are also looking forward to his visit home for Christmas. Both boys will be departing on the 26th for Campus Crusade Conferences — Bruce to Monterey, California and Tim to Chicago.

Nancy spends considerable time "caring for" and "taxiing," as well as performing her usual house and yard duties. Her Mother, Delta, is in a convalescence home several miles away, and Nancy visits her daily.

I just keep awfully busy, it seems. My job has been quite demanding this year, and the household/auto/etc. repair stuff just continues unabated.

Please stay in touch, and have a great New Year!

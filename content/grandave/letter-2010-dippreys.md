*December 19, 2010*

Hello Dip and Ingrid,

I want you to know that we very much enjoy reading your Christmas letter and keeping up with the many adventures of the Dipprey clan. You have been very good at sending along a note for many years. On the other hand we haven't done nearly as well. So this year I thought I would try to give you a quick synopsis of our family, but we don't have nearly as many adventures as you guys do.

First, a bit about our family:

Our oldest son, Tim, lives in South Pasadena in a beautiful old 1920s home. He and Karen have three children. The two oldest, Kate and Hamilton, are enrolled at Flintridge Prep, a private school which is located very close to our former home in La Canada. They are both good students and both run cross-country and track, much like their dad did. Their youngest son, Sebastian, goes to a local middle school and is a sports-nut. We do get over to South Pasadena occasionally, although not much recently, to visit them. Tim is an attorney at a downtown law firm, and Karen teaches part time at local schools.

Our other son, Bruce, is the VP of product development for an Internet startup firm that has been generously funded by one of the investment Angels. They live in Moscow, Idaho, which unfortunately is a bit difficult to get to from Arizona. Bruce and Anita have three sons and a daughter. Anita home schooled all four of these until they were old enough for middle school. They all attend a Christian school located near their home. All of these kids are very bright; the two younger boys maintaining a perfect 4.0 average so far. La Crosse is their favorite sport. The oldest, Tyler, graduated from high school in 2009 at the top of his class and did extremely well on the SATs. He was offered a full-ride scholarship in the honor college at Alabama University, and had other offers as well. He also won a cash stipend from an Alabama benefactor for students in the engineering field. In other words he is getting paid to go to college. (End of bragging, for now.)

We are still living in Scottsdale Arizona and enjoying it very much (with the exception of the very hot summer days). Our daughter, Marily, lives in a condominium about 10 min. away from us. As you may recall she is the one that was born with Spina Bifida, which affected her lower legs and thus ability to walk normally. She was able to walk, in a fashion, for many years, but recently has had to rely on a wheelchair. She was able to drive her car previously but now is learning to use hand controls. Thus she requires a bit of assistance from us, especially at the present time. She lives in a condo about 10 minutes away from us, which is quite convenient. Nancy provides most of the support for her and I help out as well.

We have both slowed up a bit now, but are basically quite healthy, for which we are thankful. Nancy keeps busy with our large house and with providing support to Marily. In the past I have been kept quite busy as a volunteer in the AARP Tax-Aide program, specializing in computers and software for free tax preparation. I was on the National Technical Committee, but last year resigned from active duty, if you will. I am still on a subcommittee, but this does not require much time. Hence I spend time doing the usual keep-up-the house stuff and messing around with my computer, which I thoroughly enjoy. My interests include aviation, since I flew military aircraft in the Air Force, World War II history, and technical topics. Unlike Dip, I tend to read and study about applied technical fields as opposed to research and mathematical fields. I am afraid I would lose interest in an eight hour CD but knowing Dip I'm sure this was fascinating information for him. By the way your trip to the Grand Canyon sounded great.

Thanks again for staying in touch. One of these days, pretty soon I hope, we will give you a call and try to get over for a visit or meet for lunch, etc.

Enjoy the Christmas season and next year as well!

Dave & Nancy

*December 18, 2010*

Hello Bob and Norma,

You folks have been very good at sending along a Christmas card every year. On the other hand I have not done well in this regard. This year I vowed I would at least send a card to good folks like you and perhaps send along a brief letter, which I have been reluctant to do in the past. (I say this because some folks overdo it with their Christmas letter.) We have especially enjoyed your notes and in some cases a nice photograph of you guys and your fine family.

First, a bit about our family:

Our oldest son, Tim, lives in South Pasadena in a beautiful old 1920s home. He and Karen have three children. The two oldest, Kate and Hamilton, are enrolled at Flintridge Prep, a private school which is located very close to our former home in La Canada. They are both good students and both run cross-country and track, much like their dad did. Their youngest son, Sebastian, goes to a local middle school and is a sports-nut. We do get over to South Pasadena occasionally, although not much recently, to visit them. Tim is an attorney at a downtown law firm, and Karen teaches part time at local schools.

Our other son, Bruce, is the VP of product development for an Internet startup firm that has been generously funded by one of the investment Angels. Bruce and Anita have three sons and a daughter. Anita home schooled all four of these until they were old enough for middle school. They all attended a Christian school located near their home in Moscow, Idaho. All of these kids are very bright; the two younger boys maintaining a perfect 4.0 average so far. The oldest, Tyler, graduated from high school in 2009 at the top of his class and did extremely well on the SATs. He was offered a full-ride scholarship in the honor college at Alabama University, and had other offers as well. He also won a cash stipend from an Alabama benefactor for students in the engineering field. In other words he is getting paid to go to college. (End of bragging, for now.)

Our daughter, Marily, lives in a condominium about 10 min. away from us. As you may recall she is the one that was born with Spina Bifida, which affected her lower legs and thus ability to walk normally. She was able to walk, in a fashion, for many years, but recently has had to rely on a wheelchair. (Bob will relate to all of this I am sure.) She was able to drive her car previously but now is learning to use hand controls. Thus she requires a bit of assistance from us at least currently. She lives in a condo about 10 minutes away from us, which is quite convenient. Nancy provides most of the support for her and I help out as well.

We have both slowed up a bit now, but are basically quite healthy, for which we are thankful. Nancy keeps busy with our large house and with providing support to Marily. In the past I have been kept quite busy as a volunteer in the AARP Tax-Aide program, specializing in computers and software for tax preparation. I was on the National Technical Committee, but last year resigned from active duty, if you will. I am still on a subcommittee, but this does not require much time. Hence I spend time doing the usual keep-up-the house stuff and messing around with my computer, which I thoroughly enjoy.

Thanks again for staying in touch. One of these days, pretty soon I hope, we will give you a call and try to get over for a visit or meet for lunch, etc.

Enjoy the Christmas season and next year as well!

Dave & Nancy

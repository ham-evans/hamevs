*December 27, 2008*

Hi Dick and Thelma,

I very much enjoyed your hand written Christmas letter. It seems like in these days we most often communicate by way of short, written e-mail messages.

It was very clever of you to ask questions of me in your letter. So I will try to answer them. First of all we spent Christmas at home in Scottsdale with Marily our daughter. We have spent a Christmas in Idaho in the past and you are right it was cold. We also got caught in a snowstorm and had to spend a night in Spokane before we caught our plane home. However, as you know, it was beautiful. Sometimes the Idaho bunch, which consists of four grandchildren plus the parents, fly down to spend Christmas with us. However normally that is a long, expensive trip to make with six people.

I do feel compelled to take this opportunity to brag a bit, however. Bruce's older son, Tyler, who will be a senior in high school next year, scored in the 99th percentile on his PSAT test, and wants to take engineering in college. Obviously he takes after his grandfather! (I wish!)

We visited Tim and Karen in South Pasadena a few days before Christmas. However this year we decided to come back home for Christmas. Like many families they have many things they do around the Christmas season and I guess were getting a bit old but like to hang around home about this time of year. Next year we are planning for both families to be here for Christmas.

You are asking about memories. Of course I have many vivid memories of high school involving you as well as many others. I was pleased to hear a little news about Duncan. I keep in touch occasionally with him by way of e-mail, but haven't done so recently. I was sorry to hear about his diabetes but I know he likes his beer. I am afraid that all of us are reaching the age that the old body starts to show its deterioration (with the exception of several of us of course).

Yes we do get to the Pasadena area for the Rose Parade but not very often these days. I'm afraid it's too easy to watch it on the television. Tim was a White Suiter for a while, but gave it up when he got tired of spending New Year's Eve out in the cold.

Dick is fortunate to be able to remain employed at least part-time. I did some consulting when I first retired however there is little opportunity for that in the Phoenix area. At this point it doesn't sound very interesting anyway, although extra income would always be welcome. I continue to stay busy providing technical support to the AARP tax aide program. I am also spending some time organizing our collection of slides, including having them converted to digital format, and other kinds of things that need to be done before I get a lot older. (After all I will be 78 years old tomorrow the 28th!) I think of you and Dick as being a lot younger.

I got to spend a couple of hours with Charles and Gwen this summer when they came out to visit with Herb Nations after Janet died. I don't see Herb very often since he always seems to be too busy or preoccupied.

I keep promising myself to give you a call when were in the Southern California area so that we could perhaps meet for lunch or some such thing, however we get busy with the family and before long we're on our way back. I'll try again to do that this coming year!

Meanwhile keep enjoying your family as we do, and tell Dick to keep sending me things of interest by way of e-mail or otherwise. You could also send a recent photo or two of you guys or your family.

Thanks again for the note and our best wishes to you guys for the new year.

Donnie (as you know me)

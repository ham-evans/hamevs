<p style="margin-bottom:0.25rem"><em>Wednesday, December 3, 2008</em></p>

Hi DeAun,

I thought I better write a letter right away before too much time elapses. I guess we really covered most everything of interest in our telephone conversation of yesterday. However I wanted to send you some snapshots of the family.

You will recognize the first two photos however the one with all the grandchildren will be less recognizable. This photo was taken last year on the front steps at Tim and Karen's house in South Pasadena, CA. All of the kids have grown since then as they are prone to do. Counting from the left numbers one, three, and five are all of Tim and Karen's family. Sebastian was 6, Hamilton 9, and Katherine 12. (Recall that Tim was anxious to name a son Hamilton after our Dad.) All these kids are very active in sports. Katie is a runner, just like her Dad was.

Bruce and Anita's crew consist of Preston 8, Jameson 11, McKenzie 13, and Tyler 16. Tyler is the one that seems especially bright. He scored so high on his Pre-SAT (college entry) exam that he got offers from some of the top colleges to attend a summer session last year. He just recently took the regular SAT, and has decided to enter an engineering program when he graduates next year, much to my delight.

I guess I will stop bragging now and get this note off to you. Perhaps the next time I write we will not have talked so recently on the phone and will be more inclined to include a bit more about us and about Marily.

Our love to you and all of your gang,

Don and Nancy

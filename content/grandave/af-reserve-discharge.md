## Transcription

### Promotion to 1st Lieutenant (Reserve) — March 7, 1957

Headquarters, Continental Air Command, Air Reserve Records Center, 3800 York Street, Denver 5, Colorado.

By Direction of the President, announcement is made of the promotion as Reserve of the Air Force of the following Second Lieutenants with Reserve assignment Hq Comd (IRS) Air Reserve Records Center Denver 5, Colorado to the grade of First Lieutenant, effective dates as indicated.

Evans, David D. AO3011940 (Perm residence 224 W Cincinnati, Las Vegas, Nevada) eff 1 Jun 56.

### Reserve Assignment — Boulder, CO, November 7, 1957

2656th Air Reserve Center, Bldg 56, Denver Federal Center, Denver, Colorado.

1st Lt Evans, David D., AO 3011940 (STANDBY)(AA)(P/A — 1507 Bluebell, Boulder, Colo) is rel fr asg 9765th Airlines Sq, 916th AirRes Gp, Boulder, Colo. Trg Cat B, Pay Group None. EDCSA: 12 Nov 57. AUTH: Para 16a, AFR 45-5, as amnd.

### Reserve at Pasadena — September 9, 1958

1stLt Evans, David D. AO 301 19 40 (290 N Euclid #307, Pasadena, Calif) is listed among officers relieved from assignment.

### Reserve Status Change — November 17, 1958

1stLt Evans, David D. AO 301 19 40 (890 N Euclid, Pasadena, Calif), 25 Nov 58. Announcement is made of Occupation Title and Code Associate Research Engineer, 0-19.03.

### Honorable Discharge — July 3, 1962

Headquarters, Air Reserve Records Center (CONAC), Denver 5, Colorado. Reserve Orders Number B-5831.

1Lt Evans David D AO3011940, 4565 Viro Road, La Canada, Cal.

By direction of the President the above named officer is relieved from assignment indicated and honorably discharged from all appointments in the United States Air Force. DD Form 256 AF will be furnished.

Assignment: Hq CONAC (ISLRS) this Center.
Effective Date: 3 Jul 62.
Authority: Paragraph 10, AFR 45-42.

### Honorable Discharge Certificate

Honorable Discharge from the Armed Forces of the United States of America. This is to certify that David D. Evans, AO 3 011 940, 1st Lt, was Honorably Discharged from the United States Air Force on the 3rd day of July 1962.

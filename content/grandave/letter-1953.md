## Transcription

Dear Mom,

How are you and Gary by now? Fine, I hope. Sorry I haven't written sooner, but from now until after finals I will be awfully busy trying to wind up the semester. Have got to finish writing a research paper among many other things. As it appears now I will probably be home Wednesday the 21st with Marshal. Will let you know later for sure about that.

How is the car running now. Probably still using a lot of gas. Will fix that when I get there. How about the deal in Nevada? Let me know any late news.

We had our annual Military Ball last night in the Student Union Building. It was a very nice affair. I got to crown the Air Force Queen and etc. Had a nice time. Got a lot of work done in the lab over the vacation as well as making a little extra money at Odeon. The new Chevrolets are out now and are they beautiful. Maybe so I can get me one towards the last of the year..ha.

Well this semester is practically over and it has certainly passed quickly. One more semester like this one and I will be graduated from this institution. Can hardly wait. This will be the last time that I will have to take final examinations. Next semester I won't have to take them. That will be nice also. Guess I will buy a class ring. Don't know how much it will cost but since I have worn my High School ring so faithfully I guess that I would also wear a University ring as well. Marshal bought himself a $27 Masonic ring yesterday. Very pretty.

Guess I had better close for now and got busy studying. Give my regards to all.

Love,
Don

P.S. — Got the shoes exchanged but tan was the only ones I could get for the blue.

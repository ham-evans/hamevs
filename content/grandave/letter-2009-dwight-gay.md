*January 4, 2009*

Hi Dwight and Gay,

Thank you for the great Christmas card, painted by a famous person, and the accompanying newsletter. We sort of gave up on sending out cards and letters although it would be very appropriate to send them to folks like you and Gay.

It sounds like you've had quite a busy year. We seem to be busy all the time but certainly didn't get around as much as you guys did. I am not surprised that both of you is involved with Liberty Heights activities since you are both very capable and of course willing to help out.

I had to check Google maps to remember where Cripple Creek was in relation to Colorado Springs. It doesn't appear to be very far away and that is a good thing. Also, It looks like you made a good decision to move Lucille out to live nearby. She must have had a very full life to make it to age 99, and at least she could share her last couple of years with you guys.

Our time seems to be divided among several activities, foremost of which is assisting our daughter, Marily who has had a walking disability since birth. Nancy draws most of the duties in this regard. I continue to spend more time than I would like on the Tax-Aide program however it does keep me gainfully occupied in doing the kinds of things that I like to do, primarily technical as you know. I am still a member of the National Technical Committee as well as technical advisor to Hal Kesler, the regional coordinator. This coming season I will serve again as an ERO at the Arizona Department of Revenue. I expect to drop one or more of these activities next season however.

I encourage you to become active in Tax-Aide since you are very good at it. I'm sure I don't need to caution you that you will need to carefully choose which activities so as not to become overloaded which is very easy to do as you know.

I also would like to stay in touch so keep me advised during the year and we will try to do the same.

Best regards to both of you,
Nancy and Dave

Dear Family,

Another year, another Christmas letter, as we try to stay in touch with each of you, which is very important to us.

1998 starts our fourth year of retirement in Scottsdale, and we are still enjoying it tremendously. We keep fully occupied doing a variety of things, focused on enjoying our extended family and pursuing our hobby of Native American art and lore that is such a part of the Southwest.

In addition to traveling to Moscow, Idaho on two occasions to visit Bruce & Anita and family, we made two trips to the Santa Fe/Albuquerque area to attend Native American shows and visit the rich collection of art museums and galleries of these two cities. Of course we routinely enjoy this kind of activity all around the Phoenix area.

Dave was invited to the Jet Propulsion Laboratory in July for the Mars Pathfinder landing activities and in September for the Mars Global Surveyor orbit insertion event. He enjoyed seeing some of his previous work at JPL come to fruition, since he was deeply involved in both planning and "selling" the current Mars program to NASA management.

This year Dave has been heavily involved in volunteer work for the "Taxaide" program. This activity, sponsored by the AARP Foundation and the IRS, performs tax services for low to middle income seniors during the tax season. Because of his computer experience, he was asked to initiate electronic filing for the Scottsdale district, which is a heavy producer for the program. This has taken considerable time to prepare courses, find donated computers, and teach classes in electronic filing, but he is enjoying it thoroughly.

We enjoy seeing Tim and Karen, and daughter Katie (2 yrs) quite often, since they live a short distance across town. They are now expecting their second child in late December. Tim is a fourth-year associate, specializing in industrial real estate transactions, at the Phoenix office of Squire, Sanders & Dempsey, an international law firm. They keep busy with Katie, church and, lately, preparing for a new arrival.

Bruce and Anita and grandchildren, Tyler (5 1/2), Kenzie (4), and Jamison (1), spent last Christmas with us during a short stay on their way to Moscow, Idaho where Bruce is on Staff of the University of Idaho. He is enjoying his new job as coordinator of the University's Summer and Extended Learning programs. Anita is now working a few hours each week as an occupational therapist at a local hospital. They are enjoying the small town atmosphere of Moscow, and continue to be active in their Church.

Marily had a busy year. She spent much of the summer furnishing and decorating her new condominium, which is just a few miles from us. She is looking forward to having her best friend Wendy move in with her in January, which will also help with the phone bills as Wendy is currently living in Washington, DC. She travels to California often to see friends and to visit her "girls" that she cared for when she worked as a nanny. She also keeps busy with church friends and activities and finds time quite often to take care of Katie.

We wish you and yours good health, Merry Christmas, and a Happy New Year. Please stay in touch.

Dave & Nancy

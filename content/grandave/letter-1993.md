Dear Family,

I seem to do my annual note a bit later each year, but here goes....

Except for the loss of Mars Observer, we had a fine year. Bruce and Anita blessed us all on November 1st with a granddaughter, McKenzie (or Kenzie as they call her). She joins Tyler, who will be two in late January. They are still living in Moscow, Idaho, performing Christian outreach work with the Campus Crusade for Christ program at the University of Idaho and Washington State University. We are looking forward to flying up there just before Christmas and spending a few days with them.

Tim and Karen are still in Tucson, Arizona, however they are excited about moving to Phoenix in late May, after Tim's graduation from law school. Tim did very well in school, received offers from several major law firms, and accepted a position in an excellent firm in Phoenix. Karen is a very creative teacher and has had a wonderful experience teaching in a private school in Tucson. She's hoping to find an equally rewarding situation in the Phoenix area.

Marily is living at home with us for a while. She has a dream job as a daytime nanny with a loving family nearby, caring for two little girls, Allison, 3, and Kirstin, 1. They keep her very busy and provide her with many wonderful anecdotes and insights to two bright little kids growing and learning. We are happy to have her around again and to share in the fun of her job.

Nancy and I are doing well. We have spent the last several months planning and watching the gutting and reconstruction of our 45 year old kitchen. Nancy and I decided on the cabinet style and the appliances and she and the decorator have made the many other decorating decisions. Unlike our usual "hands on" projects this is being handled by a very efficient contractor so about all I have to do is keep the money flowing. After five weeks of a make-shift kitchen we're looking forward to having a real one next week.

We were shocked and alarmed when we lost contact with the spacecraft that I had spent four hard years working on, however the JPL team kept working for some time to restore communications. For this reason it took a long time to realize that it was really gone, and we will likely never know what really happened. I've put this behind me (fortunately I have been in a different job for over a year anyway). However, I'm involved in working with NASA to help determine what can be done to obtain the Mars Observer data by launching subsequent spacecraft.

For some time I have been intending to retire at the end of 1994. Now I am seriously considering moving that date up to mid-year. Nancy & I are likely to relocate out of the Los Angeles area, assuming we can obtain a fair price for our home. We also expect it will take some time to make all of this happen. We'll keep you posted. Meanwhile, we hope you are all happy and in good health. Keep in Touch!

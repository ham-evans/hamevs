Hi Everyone —

Thanks for the cookies, they were great!! My roommates appreciated them too! Everything is going good, still. There's been a lot of excitement and activities around here. Unfortunately not all has been good (what happened at that McDonalds), but most has. Tuesday night the Olympic Torch came up Mission Blvd right in front of our hotel. About 20 of us stayed up until 1:30 in the morning to see it. It really wasn't that exciting, but I took a couple pictures and at least can say that I was there. My camera has been great, I was really, really pleased with all the pictures I've gotten developed so far. One of my roomies, Chuck, is getting married this Friday night so we're all kind of excited about that. Everyone has been (the girls that is) jealous over the ring he bought, it's really beautiful. Oh, don't worry, it's not to someone our age. Chuck is over 25, and just graduated from Cal Poly. Things are going really good as Assistant Director. We've had to deal with a couple discipline problems, but nothing major, which is nice. I'm really having a fun time with all the people I get to work with. Well I don't want to be late for work so I had better quit here. Hopefully, I'll give you a ring this weekend.

Love ya, Bruce

Dear Family and Friends,

This year brought about some significant changes in the Evans' Family doin's that we would like to share with you.

First of all, at mid-year Dave finally retired from the Jet Propulsion Laboratory after 36 years of service. He's hardly had time to appreciate retirement yet, however (see next paragraph on selling home!).

That's right... after 26 years on Woodfield Road, we decided to sell our home and move to Arizona, something we had discussed doing for several years prior to Dave's retirement. So we spent the summer getting the place ready to sell and the Fall keeping it spiffed up for prospective buyers. Simultaneously, we were journeying to Phoenix to locate a new home and undertaking a massive effort to get rid of many years of accumulated "things," mostly Dave's. Escrow closes on our Woodfield Road residence on the tenth of January, so we are still busy getting ready to move and get ready for Christmas.

Tim graduated from the University of Arizona Law School and accepted a position with a Phoenix firm, Squire, Sanders, & Dempsey. Karen was employed by a prestigious private school in Phoenix, and they purchased a nice patio home, also in Phoenix. We are looking forward to being located fairly close to them.

Bruce and Anita are still in service with Campus Crusade for Christ. This year they transferred from Moscow (Univ. of Idaho) to Orlando, Florida, where Bruce is a member of the Conferences Team at Crusade Headquarters. They also purchased a nice, new home in the outskirts of Orlando, so they now have room for Tyler (2 1/2) and Kenzie (1) to spread out a bit. The Airlines will be pleased to see increased traffic between Phoenix and Orlando!

Marily was accepted into an outstanding program in Hollywood where she will become skilled in makeup artistry. She feels she needs to stay in the Los Angeles area in the near term to see if she can obtain employment in the TV/Movie industry. (She has several good contacts in this business.) Needless to say, we are going to miss having her close at hand, but we'll also be contributing to the America West airline's economic viability. We also are hoping the Phoenix TV/Movie business will continue to develop so she can be located closer to us.

Hope this finds you and your families well and prosperous. Please stay in touch with us at our new address, shown on the enclosure.

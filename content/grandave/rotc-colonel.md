*Farmington Daily Times, Farmington, N.M. — Monday, September 8, 1952*

Donald Evans of Farmington has been appointed cadet colonel in the University of New Mexico Air Force ROTC for the coming school year.

As top-ranking officer of the unit, Evans will be in command of more than 700 prospective air force officers.

Col. John L. Parker, commander of the UNM unit, said Evans had been selected because of his high academic standing and his demonstrated leadership ability.

Other organizations of which Evans is a member are: The Air Force Association, Student Publication Board, Student Standards Committee, Student Senate, and he is a wing president of the Mesa Vista dormitory.

But despite this heavy extracurricular program, Evans has maintained top grades in both his air science and university courses.

Evans is the son of Mrs. Grace Owens of Farmington.

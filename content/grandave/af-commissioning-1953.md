## Transcription

### Distinguished Graduate, Air Force ROTC — May 18, 1953

Department of Air Science and Tactics, AFROTC Detachment No. 510, The University of New Mexico, Albuquerque, New Mexico.

To: Second Lieutenant David D. Evans, AO 3 011 940

The Secretary of the Air Force has authorized me to inform you that you are hereby designated a Distinguished Graduate of the Class of 1953, Air Force ROTC, University of New Mexico.

During the course of your training, you have demonstrated that you possess to an unusual degree the ability, initiative, and other leadership qualities so essential to successful performance of duty as an Air Force officer. You are deserving of the highest praise.

If you desire to make a career of the Air Force, you will be afforded special consideration for Regular appointment. A copy of this correspondence should be attached to your application whenever you do meet existing eligibility criteria and apply for Regular appointment.

Congratulations on your outstanding achievement.

John L. Parker, Colonel, USAF, PAS&T

### Certificate — June 2, 1953

Det 510, HQ AFROTC, The University of New Mexico, Albuquerque.

I certify that David Donald Evans will on 2 June 1953 successfully complete all requirements for a commission in the USAFR, under the regulations prescribed for commissioning individuals upon completion of the Air Force ROTC course of instruction. I consider him physically, mentally, morally, and professionally qualified for appointment as a Second Lieutenant, United States Air Force Reserve, and I recommend his appointment.

John L. Parker, Colonel, USAF, PAS&T

### Appointment in the Reserve — June 2, 1953

Air University, Headquarters Air Force ROTC, 435 Bell Street, Montgomery, Alabama.

Subject: Appointment in the Reserve, United States Air Force

To: 2d Lt David D. Evans, AO 3 011 940, Res-USAF, 620 S. Main St., Farmington, New Mexico

1. The Secretary of the Air Force has directed me to inform you that by direction of the President, you are tendered an appointment as a Reserve Officer in the United States Air Force, in the grade and with service number shown in address above, for an indefinite term.

2. There is enclosed herewith a form for Oath of Office which you are requested to execute and return to this office promptly. The execution and return of the required Oath of Office constitute an acceptance of your appointment. No other evidence of acceptance is required.

3. You will not perform the duties of an officer under this appointment until specifically called to active duty by competent authority.

By Command of Brigadier General De Ichelmann:
Stanley Brzoska, 1st Lt, USAF, Adjutant General

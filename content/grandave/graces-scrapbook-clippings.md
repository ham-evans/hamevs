## Three Local Boys Honored By Who's Who in American Universities

*Farmington, N.M. — Thursday, Nov. 1*

Three San Juan County boys who are seniors at the University of New Mexico will be honored by having their names included in the new Who's Who in American Colleges and Universities, Dean Lena Clauve announced this week.

They are Glen Campbell and Don Evans of Farmington, and Al Utton, of Aztec. Thirty seniors at the university were selected by organizations rather than by individual vote, Miss Clauve said. Sixteen of these were residents of New Mexico while the remaining 14 were out-of-staters.

Glen who is the son of Mr. and Mrs. Ike Campbell, 1208 Laguna Drive, is co-captain of the University of New Mexico Lobos this year and is active in athletics. He is majoring in chemical engineering and is a member of KHATALI, senior men's honorary fraternity. While a student in Farmington high he was active in football, basketball and track and was president of the student body in his senior year. His brother, Tommy E. is assistant football coach and head basketball coach here this year. He has a younger brother, Larry, who is a student in junior high.

Evans who is the son of Mrs. Grace Owens, East Aztec highway, is a top ranking ROTC Cadet Colonel in the University of New Mexico Air Force. As such he has over 700 men under him. Besides majoring in chemistry he is a member of the student publications board, the student standards committee and the student senate. He was chosen for his high academic standing and his leadership abilities.

Carroll Evans, a brother, is with the Sterling Furniture company and he has a younger brother, Gary, who is a student in junior high. Al who was born and reared in Aztec.

## 32 Air Cadets At UNM Receive Emblems of Rank

*Albuquerque*

Sixteen Albuquerque residents were among the 32 top-ranking Air Force cadets receiving their emblems of rank at a change of command ceremonies at ROTC drill last week on the University of New Mexico campus.

New Mexico residents took 27 out of the 32 top ranks, Col. John L. Parker, USAF, said. Colonel Parker is professor of air science and tactics on the UNM campus.

Cadet John Manias, Peoria, Ill., replaced Cadet **Col. D. D. Evans, Farmington** as wing commander. Other officers named at the ceremonies, who will serve the school year 1953–54, include:

W. S. Seese, Albuquerque, executive officer; Herb Nations, Albuquerque, adjutant; John A. Farris, Albuquerque, operations officer; Don Anderson, El Paso, supply officer; K. W. Michael, Albuquerque, public information officer.

## Two Las Vegans Attend Colorado Summer Sessions

*Boulder, Colo., Aug. 23* — David D. Evans, of 224 W. Cincinnati, and Mrs. Anna L. Fayle, of 1301 S. 7th, attended the University of Colorado summer session.

The session, which includes two five-week terms, annually attracts students and teachers from every state in the nation. The university is serving more than 6500 persons during the summer.

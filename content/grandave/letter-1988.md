Dear Friends,

Already it's Christmas time — the months and years even just seem to fly by. As usual, I will share with you a bit of our goings-on, as it is my custom this time of year. I have already received cards and notes from many of you, since I am getting this out late this year.

Marily (17) is a senior at La Canada High School. She is doing well in school and has a part time job. She also enjoys working in the child care center at Church. Marily just returned from a short trip to Prescott, where she visited her friend Christy. They both enjoy cooking and have been known to make a two-course meal for two, just for fun. She is looking forward to graduation and taking a graduation trip with her friend to Hawaii.

Bruce and Julie have arranged and furnished their apartment in Del Mar (near San Diego). Bruce is working at General Atomics as an administrative assistant in the legal department, and Julie continues to work at Strategic Hospital Recruiting as an occupational therapist. They are both involved in church work during their spare time and plan to become staff members with Campus Crusade for Christ in about a year. (This is the same organization that Tim was with for a couple of years.)

Tim is currently working as an attorney at a large real estate law firm in downtown Los Angeles. This job is enabling him to earn some money and giving him some exposure to the legal profession which will help him make a more permanent career decision. He is living at home for now, so our nest is not quite empty yet.

Nancy and I are keeping busy just keeping up, it seems. Nancy's mother, Delta, is still in a nursing home, not far from us. Delta is not doing very well; she has some good days and some bad ones. We do our best to keep her happy, honest, and comfortable. I am continuing as a Program Manager at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, and we keep very busy all the time. I am starting to plan a bit more seriously for retirement (in a few years) and Nancy and I look forward to some interesting possibilities.

Have a joyous New Year, and keep us posted on your happenings!

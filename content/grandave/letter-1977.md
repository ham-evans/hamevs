Dear Family & Friends,

Greetings from our family to yours: We've had a busy year, and I bet you have also. Seems as though there isn't enough time to think.

I am still an engineering manager at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory. My Section is responsible for the liquid propulsion systems on our spacecraft to Mars and Jupiter (currently), as well as an automotive research and development activity. Nancy keeps awfully busy just keeping up with the house, yard, two active boys, and Marily. Nancy is also quite active in the parent organization at Marily's school — helping raise money and that sort of thing.

Tim (15) is a sophomore at La Canada High School and is doing well in school. He has developed an interest in running. He did well on the cross-country team and is now running track. Bruce (13) has developed into an excellent student in Junior High School (but not yet quite as neat as his old man!). He still pitches well in baseball, his favorite sport.

Marily (6) is a first grader, and looks just like one with her missing-teeth grin. She is happy, pretty, and doing very well. She walks well in her braces, but recognizes her limitations. She is now learning to read — that's thanks for all.

We all wish each of you a happy holiday season and a prosperous New Year. Please let us hear from you. Note that we have a city and zip code change.

## Transcription

### Called to Active Duty — July 30, 1953

Special Orders from Headquarters, Fourteenth Air Force (CONAC), Robins Air Force Base, Georgia.

2d Lt David D. Evans is among the named AFROTC Cadets announced as Second Lieutenants of the Reserve of the Air Force and assigned to Hq, Fourteenth Air Force (MARS) Robins Air Force Base, Georgia (Non-Rated). Date of commission: 2 Jun 53. Home address: 600 E. Main St., Farmington, N.M.

### Orders to Lackland AFB — July 30, 1953

Special Orders, Fourteenth Air Force, Robins AFB, Georgia. 2d Lt David D. Evans is called to active duty and ordered to report to Lackland AFB for 3 days of processing, then assigned to 3302d Pilot Training Squadron (Contract Primary), ATRC, Spence Air Base in Moultrie, Georgia. Report date: 28 Sep 53. Travel time: 3 days.

### Primary Flight Training Graduation — May 11, 1954

Special Orders from Headquarters, 3302D Pilot Training Squadron (Contract Primary), Spence Air Base, Moultrie, Georgia.

The following named officers graduated from primary flight training. 2ndLt David D. Evans, AO3011940, is among those reassigned to Laredo Air Force Base, Texas for jet training. Duration: five months. Report date: 27 May 54.

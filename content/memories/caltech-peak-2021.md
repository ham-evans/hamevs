Summer 2021 was one of the best. So many activities and adventures. I had the great idea of going to the Sierras and climbing Caltech Peak. I had read about it online somewhere and thought it would be great to give it a go as we were in school there after all!

We planned a three day, two night backpack — up to Shepherds Pass the first day, climb the peak and back with daypacks the second day, then hike out the third. As such we brought three days of food and everything we needed, so packs were quite heavy for the climb. We got camping gear situated (maybe Sadie needed a new backpack?) and came up with a route. We went out August 27th in the afternoon and camped at the trailhead.

![Day one on the trail](/memories/caltech-peak-day-one.jpeg)

We started hiking at sunrise from the Symmes Creek trailhead and went up to Shepherds Pass that day. It was 11.63 miles with 6,045 feet of climbing and took just over 7 hours. One of the hardest days of hiking we ever had. The steep vertical with full packs made for a brutal one. We approached the basin and it was just straight uphill for the last mile or two, exposed sun the whole way, but we kept powering up.

![Hiking up to Caltech Peak](/memories/caltech-peak.jpeg)

![Camp at Shepherds Pass](/memories/caltech-peak-camp.jpeg)

We made it to the pass at about 12,000 feet in the late afternoon and set up camp. I remember making Sadie jump in the lake up there! It was freezing cold with ice sticking in but we still went for a swim and cleaned off. Had some packaged meals for dinner but I remember them being quite good actually. Then we watched an insane sunset looking west towards the valley and Sequoia.

![Sunset at Shepherds Pass](/memories/caltech-peak-sunset.jpeg)

We woke up with the sunrise, made some coffee, and got ready for summit day. We left camp relatively early and traversed down into the valley and then around to Caltech Peak.

We were probably underprepared on scouting the route from here. I had read that there was a relatively easy path up the side of Caltech Peak that you could just walk up. We approached the peak but couldn't see a clear way up. We did some scouting up the side and gave it a go traversing across and up simultaneously. We never found much of a path, and ended up on steep scree and rocks. We made it a few hundred feet up but it got progressively more sketchy and exposed.

Pretty soon after we bailed on this path and went back down to the base. We were in the basin of Caltech Peak and searched around for another way up, heading towards Forester Pass (the highest point on the PCT!), but never found a great route. Sadie was feeling a bit sketched out from the exposure on our first attempt, and we were both feeling tired.

In the end we didn't get very close to the summit and decided to bail, but got a great picture in our Caltech shirts first:

![Caltech shirts at the base](/memories/caltech-peak-shirts.jpeg)

You can see in the background it's not exactly clear where to go, and the terrain was full of rocks, scree, and pretty dodgy with lots of exposure. We were coming in from left to right but failed to make it all the way.

We went back to camp from here and had lunch and maybe even went for another swim. At this point it was only midday — around noon or so — so we hung out for a bit then eventually decided we should pack up and move down from Shepherds Pass to a lower camp (we had passed about three campgrounds on the way up).

We did just that, packing up and heading down a few miles to the first camp. We got there but were both feeling pretty good (probably from dropping from 12,000 feet in elevation to 9,000 feet). So we kept on going with the idea of camping at the second campground we saw. We went on down there but were still feeling good. The sun was setting and I think neither of us really wanted to camp out another night. We were only five or six miles from the car at this point and decided to just send it down all the way.

We got to the car at about 7 PM after hiking 20 miles, climbing 3,000+ feet with 9,000 feet of descent, and about 11 and a half hours of hiking.

![20.14 miles at the car](/memories/caltech-peak-finish.jpeg)

It's still not clear to me where we went wrong with our summit attempt. I think we needed to get on the ridge from the bottom — where we tried to cut up on the side of the mountain way too close to the summit. But it wasn't obvious while we were there for sure. Regardless we had a great time camping in the Sierras! I would love to go back and redeem ourselves sometime though. [Alternative route](https://caltopo.com/m/0D6VB) · [Summit report](https://peakery.com/caltech-peak-california/summits/378660/).

When we got to the car we were obviously exhausted but had had a great day hiking. We packed up and decided to drive home (about 4 hours) since it was only 7 PM at that point. Showers were calling our names. We stopped on the way for a burrito from a food truck which was heavenly and we watched F1 qualifying on the way down (George Russell P2 in the rain at Spa!). We took turns driving as we were so tired, but got home, showered, and enjoyed our beds for sleeping that night.

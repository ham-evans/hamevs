![View from our seats at Dodger Stadium](/memories/dodger-stadium.jpeg)

Sadie and I went to the game. My first playoff game. We sat about four rows from the top of the stadium down the third base line. I don't think we sat for most of the game. I remember we got there very early and had time to spare.

That summer we watched a ton of baseball. It felt like the Dodgers were on 24/7. We went to a handful of games, but always watching. Elliot would come over and we would watch Premier League straight into Dodgers for hours. We lived at 130 N Meridith Ave in Pasadena in the back house there with John Chapman and Holly Barnhart. John also got very into the Dodgers that summer and would watch many many games for years thereafter. Elliot lived just down the street across Colorado on Meridith.

I had a secret parking spot at Dodger Stadium. You take the Academy Road exit off the 110 and park on Academy Road somewhere right off the freeway. From here it was a ~15 minute walk to the stadium but you saved the $30+ on parking. We used this almost every game. One of the best kept secrets about the Dodgers.

I don't remember much on the game, except for the walkoff.

It was tied 1-1 in the bottom of the 9th and Chris Taylor hit a two-run walkoff home run off Cardinals closer Alex Reyes to win it 3-1, sending the Dodgers to the NLDS against the Giants. That was the loudest I've ever heard a baseball stadium. I wouldn't be surprised if it registered as an earthquake given how loud and how much jumping there was.

The Dodgers beat the Giants in the NLDS but lost to the Braves in the NLCS, but this was still one of the best experiences at a baseball game I ever had.
